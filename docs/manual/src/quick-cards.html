Quick referenceAdmin (desktop)

Every morning

  1. Dashboard: assign every request in the amber waiting to be assigned panel.
  2. Preventive, Corrective, Inspection: assign every Pending Assignment job.
  3. Service Requests: check Overdue SLA.
  4. Outside Repairs → Overdue: chase vendors.

A problem is reported

Service Requests → Action → Create Corrective Maintenance (repair) or Create Housekeeping Work Order (cleaning) → choose the person. Not needed? Dismiss with a reason.

Add an asset

Assets → Add Asset → 5 steps → on Review choose each schedule's first date. First dates can't be changed.

Add a person

Admin → Users → Add User → name, email, role → Send Invite. Not received? Resend invite.

Print QR codes

Utility → QR Codes → tick rooms or assets → Print. Rooms: 4 per A4; assets: 5 cm stickers.

Numbers

AST-Asset
ROM-Room
SR-Service request
WO-PM-Preventive job
WO-CR-Breakdown job
WO-HK-Cleaning job
INSP-Inspection
OSR-Outside repair

Shortcuts

Ctrl + K: search anything by number or name.
Initials (top right): change password, sign out.
Lifebuoy: contact support.

Rules to remember

  • Job numbers are given when someone is assigned.
  • A job can be started only once its window opens (7 days before for monthly).
  • New SLA hours apply to new requests only.
  • A failed inspection creates a repair job by itself.
  • A job with a part still out can't be completed.

Quick referenceTechnician (phone)

Preventive job

  1. Tasks → Start (or Continue).
  2. Read From the office.
  3. Take the on-site photo: the checklist unlocks.
  4. Tick every step; photo where the camera icon shows.
  5. Add part for parts fitted.
  6. Notes + completion photo → Complete & close.

Breakdown

  1. Read the reported issue and its deadline.
  2. In-house: on-site photo → problem found → solution taken → parts → after-repair photo → Complete & close.
  3. Vendor: choose the vendor, record their visit; complete once their ticket number is in.

Part or asset to a workshop

Send a part or the asset out → part or whole asset, vendor, expected return → Send out. When back: Mark received back. The job can't be completed while anything is out.

Inspections

Inspections → Start inspection → photo at the asset → PASS / FAIL each checkpoint (say what is wrong on a FAIL) → photo at the end → Submit & complete.

Scan

Round Scan button → Tap to scan → point at a room or asset QR code. Or Pick a room / Pick an asset.

Report a problem

Requests → New request → type, room, equipment, description, photo → Submit request.

"Opens on …"

Planned jobs can be started from: 1 day before (weekly), 7 days (monthly), 15 days (quarterly), 1 month (half-yearly), 2 months (annual).

Tips

  • Save as in progress often on long jobs.
  • Switch on alerts: bell → Alerts when the app is closed.
  • 08:00 every day: one Due today alert.

Quick referenceHousekeeping (phone)

Routine cleaning

  1. Cleaning tab → Start on the job.
  2. Before cleaning photo (optional).
  3. Tick each of the 5 checklist steps as you finish it. Note or Photo on a step to report something.
  4. After cleaning photo (required) and notes: damage, missing supplies, a leak.
  5. Complete sanitisation.

Cleaning someone asked for

The job shows their request and photos instead of the checklist. Clean, take the after photo, complete. They are told it is done.

Not finished?

Save as in progress and come back to it from the Cleaning tab.

Inspections

If the office gives you one: Inspections → Start inspection → photo at the asset → PASS / FAIL each checkpoint → photo at the end → Submit & complete.

Something broken?

Requests → New request → Maintenance → room, equipment, description, photo → Submit request.

Rooms

Round Scan button → scan the code on the door → check in or report a problem there.

Alerts

Bell → Alerts when the app is closed → allow. You get an alert when a cleaning job is given to you.

Quick referenceFaculty (phone)

An inspection is assigned to you

  1. Inspections tab → Start inspection.
  2. Photo at the asset: the checkpoints unlock.
  3. PASS or FAIL each checkpoint; a photo where it says Photo required.
  4. On a FAIL, say what is wrong. A repair job is raised for you.
  5. Observations + photo at the end → Submit & complete.

Report a problem

  1. Requests → New request (or Report a problem here in a room).
  2. Maintenance or Housekeeping → room → equipment.
  3. What is wrong, where, since when; add a photo.
  4. Submit request: you get an SR number and a deadline.

Follow your requests

Requests tab: status and time left. The bell tells you when one is resolved or closed.

Using a room

  1. Scan the code on the door (round Scan button).
  2. Choose the purpose → Confirm check-in.
  3. Leaving: Check out in the blue bar at the top.

Booking a room

Ask the facility office; reservations are made on the desktop.

Your account

Profile → Change password. Forgot it? Forgot password? on the sign-in page.

Notice for roomsVisitors

Something not working in this room?

Scan the QR code on the door with your phone camera.

  1. Open the link and choose Guest access.
  2. Enter your name, email and mobile number → Continue as guest.
  3. Report a problem here: describe it and add a photo. You get a ticket number.
  4. Using the room? Check in when you arrive and Check out when you leave.
