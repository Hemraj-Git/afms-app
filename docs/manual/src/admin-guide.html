Chapter 1Welcome to AssetNXG

AssetNXG (Asset Next Generation) keeps every asset, room and maintenance job of the School of Maritime Studies in one place, on the desktop for the office and on the phone for the people doing the work.

What it does

  • Asset register: every piece of equipment with its location, purchase and warranty details, documents, QR label and full history.
  • Planned maintenance and inspections: checklists that repeat on a fixed interval (weekly to annually), with the next job created automatically each time one is finished.
  • Breakdowns and requests: anyone, including visitors who scan a room's QR code, can report a problem; each report has a deadline (SLA) and becomes a job for a technician or housekeeping.
  • Rooms: reservations by the hour, check-in and check-out, and live occupancy.
  • Proof of work: photos before and after, every checklist answer, parts used and who did it, for audits.

Who uses what

RoleUsesTypical work
AdminDesktopSets everything up, adds assets and users, assigns jobs and inspections, follows requests and deadlines, runs reports. This guide.
TechnicianPhoneDoes preventive and breakdown jobs, sends parts out for repair, can do inspections.
HousekeepingPhoneCleaning jobs with before and after photos.
FacultyPhoneInspections assigned to them, reporting problems, checking in to rooms.
GuestPhone (no account)Visitors who scan a room's QR code: check in, check out, report a problem.

The phone screens are described in the separate Field App Guide. Admins work on the desktop only: an Admin who opens the phone app, for example by scanning a QR code, is taken to the same room or asset on the desktop.

Why do some jobs say "Pending Assignment" instead of a number?

A preventive or corrective job only becomes an official work order, with its WO number, when a technician is assigned. Until then it waits in the queue as Pending Assignment, so the numbers only count jobs that were actually given to someone.

Chapter 2Getting started

Signing in, your password, finding your way around the screen, and how the app tells you when something needs attention.

Signing in

Open soms.assetnxg.app in Chrome or Edge. Staff sign in with their email address and password.

Your first password

You never receive a password from anyone. When an Admin adds you, you get an invitation email from AssetNXG. Click Set your password in it and choose your own password. The link works once, for 12 hours; if it has expired, ask an Admin to send the invitation again.

A password must have at least 8 characters, with a lowercase letter, an uppercase letter, a number and a symbol such as ! @ # $ % & *. The list ticks green as you type, and the button stays off until all five are met.

Forgotten password

  1. On the sign-in page click Forgot password? and enter your email.
  2. Open the Reset your password email and click Choose a new password (valid for 12 hours).
  3. Choose a new password. It must be different from your current one.
Why can't I use the app before setting the new password?

The email link signs you in, but the app stays on Set password until a new one is saved. This makes sure nobody who opened the email link can skip the step. If you remember your old password after all, choose Cancel and sign out and sign in normally.

Changing your password

Click your initials at the top right, then Change password. Enter your current password and the new one twice. You stay signed in, here and on your phone.

A wrong current password, or a new one that is the same as now, is refused with the reason under the field.

Working with lists

  • Sort: click a column heading with a ⇅ arrow. Click again to reverse, a third time to go back. Priority sorts Critical → Low and status in workflow order.
  • Search box above each list narrows it as you type; most pages also have filters or tabs.
  • Pages: below each list, Rows per page (10, 25, 50 or 100) is remembered for that list on your computer.
  • Deleting always asks Please confirm, with Cancel selected; only the red button deletes. Things still in use (a room with assets, a template with open jobs, a vendor linked to an asset) can't be deleted, and the message says why.
  • Everything updates live: when a technician finishes a job on the phone, your screen changes within a few seconds without refreshing.

Alerts

A short chime plays when a new alert arrives (browsers only allow sound after you have clicked once on the page). In the bell panel you can:

  • turn the sound on or off for this computer;
  • switch on Alerts when the app is closed: Windows then shows a notification for a new service request even when AssetNXG isn't open. Allow notifications when the browser asks.

If the panel says notifications are blocked for this site (as above), click the padlock left of the web address, set Notifications to Allow, and reload the page.

Chapter 3Setting up your organisation

Before assets can be added, AssetNXG needs to know your locations, how equipment is classified, the checklists you use and who works with you. For SoMS most of this was loaded from your master data workbook; this chapter shows how to keep it up to date.

The order matters

Campus → Buildings → Rooms → Categories → Sub-categories (with templates) → Vendors → Users → Assets. Each step uses what the previous one created.

Campus, buildings and rooms

Organization → Campus and Organization → Building/Block list your campuses and the buildings in each. Use Add to create one and the pencil to edit. A campus with buildings, or a building with rooms, can't be deleted.

Organization → Rooms/Areas lists every room, lab, office, corridor and outdoor area, each with its own ROM number and QR code.

  1. Click Add Room/Area.
  2. Choose the building, then enter the name, floor, size in sq ft and room type, and whether it can be reserved.
  3. Save. The room gets the next ROM number and a QR code.

Manage Room Types keeps the list of types (Classroom, Laboratory, Simulator Block and so on). A type that rooms still use can't be removed.

Click Room Hub on a row to see one room: its assets, who is in it now, its reservations and its check-in history.

Categories and sub-categories

Categories are the broad groups (Electrical, IT, Furniture, Plumbing, Safety, Civil, Mechanical). Sub-categories are the kinds of equipment inside them (AC, Projector, Fire Fighting Equipment…). Each sub-category has a short code, for example ELEC-AC00.

A sub-category holds three things that every asset in it inherits:

  • Custom fields (called Metadata Fields): the extra details to record for that kind of equipment, for example Cooling Capacity (Ton) and AC Type for an AC.
  • PM templates: the preventive maintenance checklists offered when an asset is added.
  • Inspection templates: the inspection checklists offered when an asset is added.
Adding templates to a sub-category that already has assets

When you publish templates onto a sub-category that already has assets, AssetNXG asks Schedule existing assets?. Schedule now lists those assets so you can start their schedules at once, from today or from a date you pick. Later leaves them under an amber Not scheduled banner on the Preventive and Inspections pages, where you can schedule them at any time.

Maintenance and inspection templates

A template is a checklist with an interval. Utility → Maintenance Templates holds the preventive maintenance checklists (each step is a tick-box); Utility → Inspection Templates holds the inspection checklists (each step is Pass or Fail).

  1. Click Create PM Template (or Create Inspection Template).
  2. Give it a title and description and choose the interval: Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Half-Yearly or Annually.
  3. Add the steps in order. Mark a step Mandatory if it must be done, and Photo if the technician must photograph it.
  4. Save, then attach the template to the right sub-categories.
Rules
  • A template with no steps can't be scheduled.
  • A template still used by a scheduled job can't be deleted.
  • Editing a template changes future jobs only; jobs already created keep the checklist they were created with. A PM schedule keeps the interval it started with; an inspection schedule picks up a new interval from its next completion.

Vendors

Utility → Vendor List holds the suppliers and service companies: contact person, phone, email, what they supply, and AMC (annual maintenance contract) number and dates. Assets name the vendor they were bought from and, if a vendor maintains them, the AMC vendor. Technicians can also add a vendor from the phone when sending something out for repair.

Users and departments

Admin → Users lists everyone who can sign in (Registered) and every visitor who used Guest Access (Guests). Click a person to see the assets assigned to them, their room check-ins and the requests they raised.

Adding a person

  1. Click Add User.
  2. Enter the full name and email address, and choose the role (Admin, Technician, Housekeeping or Faculty) and department.
  3. Click Send Invite. The person receives the invitation email and sets their own password.

Until they do, the list shows an amber Invite pending badge and a Resend invite button. In the "assign to" lists they appear last, marked invite not accepted yet, and can't be chosen.

Rules that protect your data
  • You can't delete yourself, remove your own Admin role, or remove the last Admin.
  • A person who has raised service requests can't be deleted (their requests would lose their author). Change their role or leave them instead.
  • A visitor can't use Guest Access with a staff member's email; they are told to sign in with their password.

The Departments tab lists departments with a code and an optional head of department (chosen from registered users). A department with people in it can't be deleted.

Chapter 4Assets

An asset is any equipment you want to track and maintain. Each has an AST number, a home room, its specifications, and its own PM and inspection schedules.

An asset's status is Operational, Under Maintenance (a breakdown job is open on it) or Retired. The status changes by itself as jobs are opened and finished.

Adding an asset

Click Add Asset (on the Assets page or the dashboard). The form has five steps.

StepWhat you enter
1. Basic InformationName; category and sub-category; SLA priority; manufacturer, model and serial number; price; purchase, installation and warranty dates; Maintenance By (In House, or a Vendor with AMC dates) and the vendor it was bought from (+ Add New Vendor if it is new).
2. LocationCampus, building and room, and optionally the person it is assigned to.
3. SpecificationThe custom fields of its sub-category, and a photo.
4. DocumentsInvoices, warranty cards, manuals or certificates (PDF or images).
5. ReviewEverything once more, and the PM & inspection schedule (below).
Why does the asset have an SLA priority?

It decides how urgent a breakdown of this asset is. When anyone reports a problem with it, the request's priority is fixed to the asset's priority (Critical, High, Medium or Low), so a broken fire alarm is never filed as Low. The resolution hours for each priority are set in Configure SLA Rules (chapter 5).

The PM and inspection schedule

On the Review step, each template of the sub-category is listed with its first date, the date of the first job:

  • From the installation date: installation date + one interval. Not offered when the asset was installed too long ago.
  • From today: today + one interval.
  • Pick the date: any date from today up to two years ahead, and not before installation.

Untick a template to skip it, or use + Add PM / + Add inspection to add another (for example a Monthly and a Quarterly PM on the same AC).

First dates can't be changed after they are set

The confirmation lists each first date. After that, every later date follows the template's interval from each completion, so the schedule runs by itself. If you set a wrong first date, ask support for help rather than adding a second schedule.

Many assets at once

For a large list, use Bulk Upload on the Assets page: download the template, fill one row per asset, and upload it. For the first load of SoMS equipment, the HMS team loads your returned Assets workbook directly.

The asset page

Click an asset to open its page.

  • PM & inspection schedule shows each running schedule with its next date, and the sub-category's templates not yet scheduled. Add a schedule starts another one; a template already running on the asset can't be added twice.
  • Tabs: Basic Information, Maintenance History (every job, including outside repairs), Inspection Log, Documents and Activity Timeline (every event, newest first, with its reference number).
  • An orange Off site for repair banner appears while the whole asset is away at a workshop.
  • Use Edit to change details or the status. Retiring an asset cancels its jobs that haven't started and stops new ones being scheduled.

QR codes

Utility → QR Codes prints the codes that people scan with the phone app.

  • Room & Facility QR Codes: placards for doors and walls, four per landscape A4 sheet, headed with the School's name. Scanning one lets staff and visitors check in or report a problem in that room.
  • Asset & Equipment QR Codes: 5 cm × 5 cm stickers for the equipment. Scanning one opens the asset and its open work.

Tick the ones you need (or Select All) and click Print. Print Status shows which have been printed.

Spares and documents

Asset Management → Inventory Hub / Spares lists spare parts and stock: quantity, minimum level (low stock is flagged), store location, unit cost and vendor. A spare unit can be Deployed as an asset when it is installed; its documents move with it.

Utility → Document Library holds all uploaded files (invoices, manuals, certificates) and lets you link them to assets or spares. Files up to 25 MB; photos up to 5 MB on assets.

Chapter 5Service requests

A service request is a problem someone reported: a breakdown (Maintenance) or something to clean (Housekeeping). Your job is to turn each one into work, quickly enough to meet its deadline.

Where requests come from

  • Staff on the phone app (New request, or Report a problem here in a room).
  • Visitors who scan a room's QR code and use Guest Access.
  • Admins, with Create Service Request on the desktop.

When a new request arrives, Admins get a bell alert with a chime, and the dashboard shows it in the amber new service requests waiting to be assigned panel.

The Service Request Desk

StatusMeans
OpenNew; nobody is working on it yet.
In ProgressA work order has been made from it and assigned.
ResolvedIts work order was completed (set automatically).
ClosedDismissed without work, with a reason.
EscalatedFlagged for urgent attention.

The person who raised the request is told on their phone when it is escalated, resolved (with your resolution note) or closed.

Turning a request into a job

  1. Click Action on the request (or Assign on the dashboard).
  2. Read the description, the asset and the deadline.
  3. Click Create Corrective Maintenance for a breakdown, or Create Housekeeping Work Order for cleaning. Choose the technician or housekeeping person and add any instructions; they appear on that person's phone under From the office.
  4. The request becomes In Progress and shows the work order number. When the job is completed on the phone, the request becomes Resolved by itself.

If no work is needed (a duplicate, or already fixed), click Dismiss and give the reason; the request is closed.

SLA: resolution deadlines

Each priority has a number of hours to resolve a request. A request's deadline is its time of reporting plus those hours; past it, the request counts as Overdue SLA.

To change the hours, click Configure SLA Rules (on Service Requests or Work Orders), edit and save. The defaults are Critical 4 h, High 12 h, Medium 24 h and Low 48 h.

Why didn't the existing requests change?

New hours apply to requests raised from now on. Requests already raised keep the deadline they were given, so a change of rules never makes yesterday's work late or on time after the fact.

Chapter 6Work orders

A work order is a job given to someone. There are three kinds: Preventive (planned maintenance from a schedule), Corrective (a breakdown or defect) and Housekeeping (cleaning).

The Work Orders hub

Tags beside a job show extra state: Part out for repair, With vendor, or a window note (see below). View Details opens the full job.

Create Work Order makes a one-off job: choose the kind, title, asset, room, the person, the priority and the due date, and add the scope. A one-off preventive job does not repeat.

Preventive maintenance

Maintenance → Preventive shows the next job of every PM schedule.

  1. A job arrives as Pending Assignment. Click Assign Technician; it then gets its WO-PM number and appears on the technician's phone.
  2. The technician does it on the phone: an on-site photo first, then each checklist step, then a completion photo.
  3. When it is completed, the next job is created automatically, one interval after the completion date, waiting for assignment.
Why can't a job be started early? (the "window")

A planned job can be started only from a set time before its due date, so a monthly service isn't done twice in a few days. The window opens 1 day before for weekly jobs, 7 days for monthly, 15 days for quarterly, 1 month for half-yearly and 2 months for annual. The list shows Window Open or Opens <date>.

Every morning at 08:00 each technician and inspector with jobs due that day gets one Due today alert listing them.

Corrective maintenance (breakdowns)

Maintenance → Corrective lists breakdown jobs. Each comes from a service request or from a failed inspection (created automatically, with what failed in its issue).

Assigning a technician gives the job its WO-CR number and sets the asset to Under Maintenance. On the phone the technician records the problem found, the solution and parts used, with photos; or hands the job to a vendor (their ticket number, technician and cost are recorded). Completing it sets the asset back to Operational and the request to Resolved. You get a bell alert when a job is handed to a vendor.

Housekeeping

Maintenance → Housekeeping lists cleaning jobs: routine cleaning of a room (a 5-step room checklist with before and after photos), or cleaning raised from a request (the request and its photos are shown instead of the checklist).

Job details: the technician's record

View Details shows everything recorded on a job: who has it, where, why, the outside repair box, the office's instructions, and what the technician recorded (photos, each checklist step with its note and photo, problem found, solution, parts and notes). Anything not filled in reads Not recorded.

Outside repairs

When a part, or the whole asset, has to go to an outside workshop during a breakdown job, the technician records it on the phone (or you do, in the job details): what is going, to which vendor, the expected return date (ETD), gate pass and estimated cost. It gets an OSR number.

  • The job can't be completed while anything is still out: record the return first (date, outcome, actual cost and the job sheet).
  • Past its ETD an item shows Overdue return, and at 09:00 the next morning the Admins and the technician get one alert. Use Change ETD if the vendor gives a new date.
  • The dashboard's Out for Repair tile counts everything currently away.

Chapter 7Inspections

Inspections are scheduled Pass/Fail checks of an asset, for safety and compliance. They are created from inspection templates, just like PM jobs, and can be done by technicians or faculty.

Assigning an inspector

  1. Click Assign Inspector on a Pending Assignment inspection.
  2. Choose the person and add any instructions for the field.
  3. Click Confirm Inspector Assignment. It appears on their phone; Change reassigns it later.

The same window rule as preventive maintenance applies: an inspection can be performed only once its window is open.

Results

  • PASS: every checkpoint passed. The next inspection is scheduled one interval later, and the asset stays Operational.
  • FAIL (Corrective Created): at least one checkpoint failed. A corrective work order Defect from INSP-… is created automatically, describing what failed, and waits on the Corrective page for a technician. The next inspection is also scheduled.

View Details shows the photos (at the start and at the end), each checkpoint's answer, what was wrong for each failed one, and Not answered for any left blank.

The compliance score

Compliance = inspections passed ÷ inspections completed × 100. Until the first inspection is completed there is nothing to score, and it shows "—". The dashboard labels the score Outstanding at 90% and above, Good from 75%, and Needs Action below that.

Chapter 8Rooms in use

Rooms can be booked in advance, and people check in when they arrive, so the office always knows which rooms are in use and by whom.

Reservations

  1. Click Book Reservation (or a free slot in the grid).
  2. Choose the room, the start and end date, and any weekdays to skip (for example Saturday and Sunday for a term-long booking).
  3. Choose one or more hourly slots, the person it is for (their department is filled in), and the purpose.
  4. Save. Every slot is checked for clashes first; slots already taken are skipped and you are told which.

All Bookings lists every reservation, with search and cancel. Only rooms marked reservable can be booked.

Check-ins and occupancy

People check in to a room from the phone (by scanning the room's QR code, or picking the room) and check out when they leave. The room then shows Occupied with the person's name on the Rooms page, in its Room Hub and on the dashboard's Occupied Rooms tile, and changes back to Available when the last person checks out. Anyone still checked in at midnight is checked out automatically.

Each room's Room Hub, and the Room Access & Activity report, keep the full check-in history (who, when, purpose).

Chapter 10Good practice and troubleshooting

A daily routine for the facility office

  1. Morning: open the dashboard. Assign every request in the amber panel, and every Pending Assignment job on the Preventive, Corrective and Inspections pages.
  2. Check Overdue SLA on Service Requests and the Overdue tab of Outside Repairs; chase or escalate.
  3. During the day: answer bell alerts (vendor handovers, items sent out).
  4. Weekly: look at the Not scheduled banners on Preventive and Inspections, low-stock spares, and the Technician Workload report.
  5. Monthly: export the Preventive, Corrective, Inspection and SLA reports for your records.

Common questions

QuestionAnswer
A new person never received the invitation.Check the spam folder. In Users, click Resend invite; the new link is valid for 12 hours.
Someone can't sign in.Ask them to use Forgot password?. Their role decides where they land: Admins on the desktop, everyone else in the phone app.
I can't find a person in the "assign to" list.They haven't accepted their invitation yet (shown last, greyed), or they have a different role (cleaning jobs list Housekeeping staff only).
A job can't be started on the phone.Its window hasn't opened yet (chapter 6), or the on-site photo hasn't been taken.
A breakdown job can't be completed.Something is still out for repair; record its return first. In-house jobs also need the solution and the after-repair photo.

An asset has no PM jobs.Its sub-category had no templates when it was added. Use Add a schedule on the asset page, or the Not scheduled banner.
The screen says "You're offline".The connection dropped. Nothing you saved is lost; the page catches up when you are back online.

Getting help

Email support@assetnxg.app (also under the lifebuoy icon in the header). Please include your name, the page, the record number (AST, SR, WO…) and, if possible, a screenshot.
