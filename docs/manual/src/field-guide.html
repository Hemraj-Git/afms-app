Chapter 1Getting started

The field app runs in your phone's browser. Install it on your home screen once, and it opens like any other app.

Your account

The facility office adds you to AssetNXG. You then get an invitation email: tap Set your password and choose a password (valid for 12 hours, once). A password needs at least 8 characters with a lowercase letter, an uppercase letter, a number and a symbol (! @ # $ % & *). The rules tick green as you type.

Install the app on your phone

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open soms.assetnxg.app in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Open AssetNXG from the new icon on your home screen.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open soms.assetnxg.app in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (the square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open AssetNXG from the new icon. Alerts on an iPhone only work from this icon.

Your tabs

The bar at the bottom changes with your role. The round Scan button in the middle is always there.

A small number on a tab is how much open work is waiting there. The bell at the top right lists your alerts: a job or inspection assigned to you, an update on a request you raised, or a reminder. Tap one to open what it is about.

Your phone's back button closes the screen you are on. If you have typed something or taken a photo that isn't saved yet, the app asks before throwing it away.

Alerts when the app is closed

Tap the bell, then switch on Alerts when the app is closed and allow notifications. Your phone then buzzes when work is assigned to you, even when AssetNXG is closed. On an iPhone, install the app first (above). Every morning at 08:00 you also get one Due today alert listing the jobs and inspections due that day.

Chapter 2Technicians: your tasks

The Tasks tab lists every job assigned to you: preventive maintenance (planned) and breakdowns (something reported broken).

The four cards count your open work orders, PM due, breakdowns and jobs completed. Tap a card, or a filter (All, Preventive, Corrective, Completed), to narrow the list.

Each job shows its number, kind and priority, the asset and room, when it is due, and its status. Tap Start on a new job, or Continue on one you have started.

"Opens on …": why can't I start this job yet?

Planned jobs can only be started a set time before their due date, so a service isn't repeated too soon: 1 day before for weekly jobs, 7 days for monthly, 15 days for quarterly, 1 month for half-yearly and 2 months for annual.

Doing a preventive maintenance job

  1. Read the job card and anything under From the office.
  2. 1. Start on site: take the photo with the asset. The checklist stays locked until you do.
  3. 2. Checklist: tick each step as you do it. Steps marked with a camera need a photo.
  4. 3. Parts used: Add part for each part you fitted.
  5. 4. Finish: write what you did and any readings, and take the completion proof photo.
  6. Tap Complete & close. To stop half-way and come back later, tap Save as in progress.

When you complete a job, the next one in its schedule is created by itself for the office to assign.

Fixing a breakdown

  1. Read the Reported issue: who reported it, when, and by when it must be fixed.
  2. Who is fixing it? Choose In-house if you are, or Vendor if an outside company is.
  3. In-house: take the on-site photo, then fill in the problem found, the solution taken (required) and any parts, and take the after repair photo.
  4. Tap Complete & close. The asset goes back to Operational, and the person who reported the problem is told it is resolved.

Handing a job to a vendor

Choose Vendor, pick the vendor (the asset's maintenance vendor is chosen for you if it has one), and record their visit: ticket or job number, their technician's name and phone, visit date, cost, remarks and a photo of their job sheet. Save as in progress tells the office; the job can be completed once the vendor's ticket number is entered.

Sending a part or the asset out for repair

If something has to go to a workshop, tap Send a part or the asset out:

  1. Choose A part (and name it, e.g. "Motherboard") or The complete asset.
  2. Choose the repair vendor (or Add a vendor not in the list), the date sent, the expected return date, and optionally a gate pass number, estimated cost and a photo.
  3. Tap Send out. It gets an OSR number and the job shows Part out for repair.
  4. When it comes back, tap Mark received back: the date, the outcome (Repaired, Replaced by vendor or Not repairable), the actual cost and the job sheet photo.
The job can't be completed while anything is still out.

Record the return first. If the vendor gives a new return date, use Change expected date; past the expected date, you and the office get an "Overdue return" alert the next morning.

Chapter 3Housekeeping: cleaning

The Cleaning tab lists the cleaning jobs assigned to you: routine cleaning of a room, or cleaning someone asked for.

Each job shows the room, the building, what it is for and when it is due. Tap Start to open it.

Routine cleaning

  1. 1. Before cleaning: a photo of the room (optional).
  2. 2. Checklist: tick each of the five steps as you finish it. Use Note or Photo on a step to report something.
  3. 3. After cleaning: the after photo (required) and any notes, such as damage, missing supplies or a leak.
  4. Tap Complete sanitisation, or Save as in progress to finish later.

Cleaning from a request

When someone reported something to clean, the job shows their request and its photos instead of the five-step checklist. Clean, take the after photo, and complete it; the person who reported it is told it is done.

Chapter 4Inspections

An inspection is a Pass/Fail check of an asset, such as a fire extinguisher or a safety system. The office assigns them to technicians, housekeeping staff or faculty.

The cards count inspections to do, overdue, passed and defects found. Tap Start inspection on one that is due.

  1. 1. Start at the asset: take the photo at the asset. The checkpoints unlock.
  2. 2. Checkpoints: tap PASS or FAIL for each one. A checkpoint marked Photo required needs a photo. For a FAIL, say what is wrong.
  3. 3. Finish: add any observations and take the photo at the end.
  4. Tap Submit & complete. The result is shown straight away.
What happens after a FAIL?

A repair job ("Defect from INSP-…") is created automatically for the office to give to a technician, with what you reported. You don't need to raise a separate request.

Chapter 5Scan: rooms and assets

Every room has a QR code on its door, and every asset a QR sticker. Scanning one opens it in the app.

  1. Tap the round Scan button, then Tap to scan. Allow the camera the first time.
  2. Point the camera at the QR code. The room or asset opens.

No code to hand? Use Pick a room or Pick an asset and search by name or number, or choose a building.

An asset

Shows its details, where it is, and its open jobs and inspections.

A room

Shows the building, floor, type and whether it is in use, the equipment in it, and two actions: checking in and reporting a problem.

Checking in and out of a room

  1. Open the room (scan its door code).
  2. Under Check in, choose the purpose (Class / lecture, Lab session, Meeting, Visit / tour, Maintenance or Other) and add details if you like.
  3. Tap Confirm check-in. A blue Active in: bar stays at the top of the app.
  4. When you leave, tap Check out in that bar.

The office sees the room as Occupied while you are in it. If you forget to check out, you are checked out automatically at midnight.

Chapter 6Reporting a problem

Something broken, unsafe or dirty? Report it in the app; it goes straight to the facility office with a deadline.

  1. Tap New request on the Requests tab, or Report a problem here in a room.
  2. Choose Maintenance (something to repair) or Housekeeping (something to clean).
  3. Choose the room. For maintenance, then choose the equipment in that room that needs attention, if the room has any registered.
  4. Write the description: what is wrong, where exactly, since when. A title is optional.
  5. Add a photo if it helps, and tap Submit request. You get a ticket number (SR-…).
Why can't I change the priority?

For equipment, the priority is fixed by how critical that equipment is (set by the office), so every report about it is handled with the same urgency. It also sets the deadline shown under the priority.

Following your requests

The Requests tab lists the requests you raised, newest first, with their status and the time left before the deadline. Filter by Open, Resolved or Closed, and tap one to see its progress.

The bell tells you when your request is escalated, resolved (with what was done) or closed (with the reason).

Chapter 7Your profile

The Profile tab shows your name, role, department and contact details, and:

  • Change password: your current password, then the new one twice. You stay signed in.
  • Alert settings for this phone.
  • Sign out. Signing out also stops alerts on this phone until you sign in again.

Forgot your password? On the sign-in page tap Forgot password?, enter your email, and open the link in the email (valid for 12 hours). You must choose a new password before using the app.

Chapter 8Visitors (guest access)

Visitors, contractors and anyone without an account can still check in to a room and report a problem, by scanning the QR code on the room's door.

  1. Scan the QR code on the room door with the phone camera and open the link.
  2. The sign-in page opens on Guest access and names the room you scanned.
  3. Enter your full name, email and mobile number, and tap Continue as guest.
  4. The room opens: check in, or report a problem there. Your requests are listed on the Requests tab, also on later visits with the same email.
Staff can't use guest access

An email that belongs to a staff account is refused; staff sign in with their password instead.

Chapter 9Questions and tips

QuestionAnswer
The checklist is greyed out.Take the on-site photo (or the photo at the asset) first; it unlocks the checklist.
Complete & close doesn't work.Something required is missing: the completion or after-repair photo, the solution taken, a vendor's ticket number, or a part still out for repair. The message says which.
A job I expected isn't in my list.It may not be assigned to you yet; ask the office. Completed jobs are under the Completed filter.
The camera doesn't open.Allow camera access for the site in your browser settings, then try again. You can always use Pick a room instead.
I get no alerts.Switch on Alerts when the app is closed from the bell, allow notifications, and on an iPhone use the installed app.
The signal dropped while I was working.Your work on screen stays; save again when you are back online. Use Save as in progress often on long jobs.
Something isn't right.Tell the facility office, or email support@assetnxg.app with the job or request number and a screenshot.
Good habits
  • Take photos that show the asset's label or the room, so anyone can see where the work was done.
  • Write the solution as you would explain it to the next person who repairs this asset.
  • Check out of rooms when you leave.
