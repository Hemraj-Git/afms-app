First day: secure your account

  1. Open the mailbox of maintenance.soms@cutm.ac.in and find the invitation from AssetNXG Admin (no-reply@assetnxg.app), subject You're invited to AssetNXG — School of Maritime Studies. If it isn't in the inbox, check the spam or junk folder.
  2. Click Set your password in the email to accept the invitation, and choose your password: at least 8 characters, with a lowercase letter, an uppercase letter, a number and a symbol. The link works once, for 12 hours; if it has expired, ask HMS - Digital Solutions to send a new one.
  3. The app opens on the dashboard. From now on, sign in at soms.assetnxg.app with this email and your password. Forgotten it? Use Forgot password? on the sign-in page.
  4. Admin → Users → Add User: invite at least one more Admin from your team, so you are never left without one, and check your own name and phone number in the list.
Add no-reply@assetnxg.app to your contacts

Invitations, password resets and other AssetNXG emails come from this address. Adding it to your contacts, or marking it "not spam", helps them reach the inbox, also for the staff you invite.

Go-live checklist

Work through these in order. Each points to the chapter of the Administrator Guide (AG) or Field App Guide (FG) that explains it.

  1. 1. Check the loaded locations and classificationsOpen Rooms/Areas, Categories and Sub-Categories and check names, floors and custom fields. Correct anything that is wrong now, before assets are added. (AG 3)
  2. 2. Create your maintenance and inspection templatesOne checklist per kind of job, with its interval and the steps your technicians follow (for example Split AC – Monthly PM, Fire Extinguisher – Monthly Inspection). Attach each to its sub-categories. (AG 3)
  3. 3. Add your vendorsSuppliers and service companies, with AMC details where you have a contract. (AG 3)
  4. 4. Set the SLA hoursHow many hours each priority has to be resolved: Service Requests → Configure SLA Rules. Defaults: Critical 4, High 12, Medium 24, Low 48. (AG 5)
  5. 5. Invite your staffTechnicians, housekeeping staff and faculty, each with the right role and department. They receive an email from AssetNXG Admin (no-reply@assetnxg.app) to set their own password; the link is valid for 12 hours. Tell them to check the spam folder if it doesn't arrive. (AG 3)
  6. 6. Add your assetsFill in the Assets workbook (one tab per category) and return it to HMS for loading, or add assets one by one with Add Asset. Give each asset its SLA priority and, on the Review step, the first date of each PM and inspection. (AG 4)
  7. 7. Print and fix the QR codesUtility → QR Codes: room placards on every door (4 per A4), asset stickers on the equipment (5 cm). Put the visitor notice from the Quick Reference Cards beside the placards in rooms that visitors use. (AG 4)
  8. 8. Set up the phonesEach field user installs the app on their home screen, signs in, and switches on Alerts when the app is closed. A 15-minute walk-through with the Field App Guide is enough. (FG 1)
  9. 9. Switch on desktop alertsIn the bell panel, switch on Alerts when the app is closed on the office computers, so new requests are seen at once. (AG 2)
  10. 10. Run a test togetherOne person reports a problem from the phone; the office turns it into a job and assigns it; the technician completes it with photos; everyone sees the request become Resolved. (AG 5, FG 2)

The first weeks

  • Every morning, assign new requests and Pending Assignment jobs (Administrator Guide, chapter 10).
  • Watch the Not scheduled banners on the Preventive and Inspections pages: they show assets whose sub-category has templates but no schedule yet.
  • After a month, look at the Service Requests & SLA and Technician Workload reports, and adjust SLA hours or team sizes if needed.
